About Dynamic Recipes

An early web-development project exploring responsive layouts, recipe instructions, printable pages, and adjustable portions.

Choose original, half, or double quantities on any of the three recipe pages. The controls run entirely in your browser.

This refreshed demo removes the old analytics and visitor-tracking experiments. It has no accounts, database, or contact form.
