BEHIND THE RECIPES / THE PROJECT

Made to learn.
Kept for the joy.

A small collection with a hands-on story.

Dynamic Recipes began as Andres Macias’s web-development coursework: an exploration of responsive layouts, recipe instructions and interactive portion controls.

This new edition brings the same three recipes into a colorful food-magazine layout. Andres directed the redesign and collaborated with AI on the interface, implementation and testing.

Choose original, half or double quantities. Open and close the directions as you cook. Print a clean copy to keep beside the stove.

The demo runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, database, visitor tracking or paid services.
