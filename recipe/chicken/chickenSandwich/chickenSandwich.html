Fried Chicken Sandwich

Golden chicken, a little sriracha heat and pickles piled onto a soft roll.

Prep
15 min
Cook

Difficulty
Medium
Servings
4

Cooking times stay the same when portions change. Adjust pan size or cook in batches as needed.

MAKE IT YOUR SIZE

Ingredients

Adjust portions

Original recipe · 4 servings

Ingredient list ↕
  • 1 1/2cups of all-purpose flour
  • 1egg
  • 1/4cup ranch dressing mix
  • 2boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1cup vegetable oil
  • 1/2cup of mayo
  • 1/4cup sriracha sauce
  • 4ciabatta bread rolls
  • 1jar of dill pickle chips
ONE STEP AT A TIME

Let’s make it.

01 Step 1+

Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).

02 Step 2+

Place 1/2 cup flour in a small bowl.

03 Step 3+

Beat egg in a shallow bowl with a fork.

04 Step 4+

Place 1 cup flour in a bowl; mix in ranch dressing.

05 Step 5+

Split chicken breasts to create 4 pieces. Coat each piece with plain flour, dip in egg, and coat with flour-ranch mixture. Place on a wire rack set over a rimmed baking sheet.

06 Step 6+

Heat oil in a large saucepan until almost boiling. Add 2 pieces chicken; fry until lightly golden, turning once, about 5 minutes. Return chicken to the wire rack. Repeat with the remaining chicken in batches.

07 Step 7+

Bake chicken in the preheated oven until juices run clear, about 10 minutes.

08 Step 8+

Mix mayonnaise and sriracha sauce together in a small bowl. Spread on rolls. Place 4 pickle slices on each roll. Place 1 piece of chicken on each sandwich.
