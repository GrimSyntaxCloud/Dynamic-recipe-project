Crockpot Chili

A slow-simmered bowl of beans, warming spices and all the good toppings.

FOOD
ONLY ✳

Original coursework recipe credit: Homer J.

THE GOOD STUFF
Prep
25 min
Cook
6 hours
Difficulty
Medium
Servings
6

Cooking times stay the same when portions change. Adjust pan size or cook in batches as needed.

MAKE IT YOUR SIZE

Ingredients

Adjust portions

Original recipe · 6 servings

Ingredient list ↕
  • 2tbsp. cooking oil
  • 1cup onion
  • 1cup chopped peppers
  • 4tbsp. chili powder
  • 1tsp. hot chili powder (optional)
  • 1lb ground beef or chicken
  • 2cans red beans
  • 2cans kidney beans
  • 2cans tomato puree
  • 2cans tomato sauce
  • 1cup shredded cheese (optional)
  • 1/2cup sour cream (optional)
ONE STEP AT A TIME

Let’s make it.

01 Step 1+

Heat cooking oil in a 2 quart skillet.

02 Step 2+

Saute onions and peppers for 5 minutes.

03 Step 3+

Add spices and stir for 30 seconds.

04 Step 4+

Add ground meat and cook until browned. Approximately 15 minutes.

05 Step 5+

Pour contents of skillet into 3 quart crock pot.

06 Step 6+

Rinse beans and place in crockpot.

07 Step 7+

Open and pour tomato puree and sauce into crockpot.

08 Step 8+

Cover crockpot and cook on low for 6 hours.

09 Step 9+

Serve into individual bowls and top with sour cream and cheese, if desired.
